About Me

Hey, I'm Samiksha Sahu, a Software Engineer with a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Vishwakarma University, Pune.

I have professional experience in software engineering and data engineering, having worked with enterprise teams at Syngenta and Eaton. At Syngenta, I worked on Python-based ETL pipelines, AWS serverless workflows, and enterprise data integrations, helping automate data collection and streamline reporting processes.

My experience at Eaton introduced me to the world of Master Data Management (MDM), where I gained exposure to enterprise data integration, transformation, data quality, and technologies including Snowflake, Microsoft Azure, Reltio, and Palantir Foundry.

I enjoy working at the intersection of software development, data engineering, and intelligent automation. I'm particularly interested in building scalable solutions, exploring AI-driven applications, and continuously experimenting with technologies that solve real-world problems.

Beyond technology, I enjoy dancing, playing badminton, following cricket, and reading books. I believe in continuous learning, staying curious, and finding opportunities to grow both personally and professionally.
